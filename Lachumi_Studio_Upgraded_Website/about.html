About Us

Creative work with a personal touch.

Lachumi Studio was founded with a simple yet powerful mission – to preserve life's most beautiful moments with creativity, care, and excellence. Based in Colombo, the studio serves individuals, families and businesses seeking professional visual storytelling.


Our work includes photography, videography, web designing, portrait sessions, promotional videos, photo editing and cinematic videos. Every project is shaped around the client's vision.


Our team brings together photographers, videographers and editors who explore new techniques and technologies while keeping the work personal and modern.


We have covered weddings, birthdays, school programs and religious functions, and continue to grow through the trust of our clients.

Our Journey

5 Years of Legacy

2021 — 2026

From a single vision to a growing creative legacy.

Lachumi Studio began its journey in 2021 , driven by a passion for photography, creativity and preserving meaningful moments. Over five years, that vision has grown into a creative studio bringing together photography, videography, editing, design, digital media, marketing and event-related services.
